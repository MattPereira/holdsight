# Trading in the Zone

## Five Fundamental Truths

1. Anything can happen
2. You don't need to know what happens next to make money
3. Within any defined edge, wins and losses are still random
4. An edge is only a higher probability, not certainty
5. Every moment in the market is unique


## Seven Principles of Consistency
1. I objectively identify my edges
2. I predefine the risk of every trade
3. I completely accept the risk before taking a trade
4. I act on my edges without reservation or hesitation
5. I pay myself as the market makes money available
6. I continually monitor my susceptibility for making errors
7. I understand the absolute necessity of these principles of consistent success and, therefore, I never violate them.
